# Concepts

> Shared domain vocabulary for this project — entities, named processes, and status concepts with project-specific meaning. Seeded with core domain vocabulary, then accretes as ce-compound and ce-compound-refresh process learnings; direct edits are fine. Glossary only, not a spec or catch-all.

## Landing walkthrough

### Archetype
A buyer-segment persona the landing page tailors its narrative to — currently the certification body, the partner-program operator, and the cohort-training founder. Selecting an Archetype branches the Walkthrough's steps, copy, and Step Demos.

### Walkthrough
The interactive, Archetype-tailored narrative that takes a visitor through the first six months of adopting the product, one step at a time, for the Archetype they pick.

### Step Demo
A small interactive widget mounted under a single Walkthrough step that makes that step's claim executable rather than merely stated. A step optionally names one; the named demo is resolved from a registry at render time.
