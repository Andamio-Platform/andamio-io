[Blink Labs](https://blinklabs.io/) has always looked ahead with ambition: accelerate the adoption of [Golang](https://go.dev/) within the Cardano ecosystem, build tools that inspire developers, and create a lasting technical legacy. But turning that vision into action required more than good ideas: it demanded structure, community, and continuity. That’s where their journey with *Andamio* began.
![cover image](/customer/blink-labs/blinklabs-image-1.png)

## **Challenge**
From the start, *Blink Labs* faced a double challenge:

- Technical and cultural adoption: Golang wasn’t (yet) the go-to language for most developers in Cardano, so convincing the community was a hurdle in itself.
- Building concrete tools: to show Golang’s strength in the ecosystem, they needed to prove it could power CLIs, libraries, and robust documentation. It was a “prove it to believe it” cycle.
- Without a visible open-source ecosystem, their impact risked being symbolic rather than lasting.

## **Solution**
*Blink Labs* and *Andamio* designed a plan that combined learning, action, and legacy:
- They organized weekly live open coding sessions (#buildinginpublic), open to the community. These weren’t just talks — they were real build sessions.
- Over several months, those sessions produced tangible results: functional CLIs, Golang libraries, technical documentation, and more — all released as open-source artifacts.
- Every step of the journey — successes, missteps, and discoveries — was captured in educational format inside the *Andamio Learning Platform*. The result was a living course, not just a static resource.
- That course didn’t remain passive content: *it became the foundation of an onboarding pathway for Golang developers in the Cardano ecosystem*. Anyone wanting to contribute now had a clear entry point.

The work was not only about building tools: it was about building community and knowledge.
![cover image](/customer/blink-labs/blinklabs-image-2.png)

## **Result**
- *Blink Labs* went from being a lone voice advocating Golang to becoming an active development reference within Cardano.
- The tools built gained real users, forks, and adoption — they work in production.
- The *Andamio* course became a unique entryway for new Golang developers to Cardano, speeding up talent onboarding with clear paths.
- Most importantly: the journey left behind a technical, documentary, and social legacy — not just a finished product.

## **Conclusion**
The value of *Blink Labs’* journey with *Andamio* lies not simply in “what they built together,” but in how they built it: step by step, with shared learning and openness. *Andamio* was not the flashy protagonist; it was the critical bridge that enabled *Blink Labs* to turn their Golang vision into tangible tools, a real community, and a legacy that others can now build on. This project proves that with the right platform, ideas with potential can become pathways others follow.
