[Mesh](https://meshjs.dev/) set out to make building on Cardano simple, elegant, and accessible. As an open-source TypeScript SDK, it provides everything developers need—from wallet connections and transaction builders to smart contract support and UI components. Quickly, it became clear that *Mesh* was more than just code: *it was a gateway to unlocking Web3 innovation.*
![cover image](/customer/meshjs/meshjs-image-1.png)

## **Challenge**
Even the strongest developer toolkit can falter without a community behind it. For *Mesh*, the early days highlighted a familiar open-source risk: without contributors to maintain, extend, and advocate for the tools, the project’s long-term survival would be uncertain. The question wasn’t only how to deliver high-quality code, but how to inspire a developer ecosystem to adopt and grow with it.

## **Solution**
In collaboration with *Andamio*, *Mesh* launched weekly live-coding sessions: open spaces where anyone could learn, build, and experiment in real time. Those sessions evolved into structured learning modules on *Andamio’s Learning Platform*. The resulting course now anchors an onboarding pathway into Cardano development via the *Andamio Contribution Platform*, giving aspiring contributors a clear entry point.
![cover image](/customer/meshjs/meshjs-image-2.png)

## **Result**
Today, *Mesh* stands as one of the most widely adopted toolkits in the Cardano ecosystem, powering wallets, dApps, and services with consistent weekly downloads. Their community is an active network of contributors who keep *Mesh* interoperable, production-ready, and aligned with Cardano standards (CIPs).

## **Conclusion**
*Mesh*’s story shows that open-source success is built on more than technical excellence. By combining a world-class SDK with intentional pathways for community growth, they turned a powerful tool into a living ecosystem—one where developers aren’t just consumers, but collaborators shaping the future of Cardano.
